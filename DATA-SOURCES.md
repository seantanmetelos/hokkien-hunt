# Directory provenance and caveats

Initial directory of 20 publicly listed Singapore fried Hokkien prawn mee stalls, based on the prior Hokkien Hunt prototype prepared 13 October 2026. Some entries were referenced in https://www.misslobang.com/article/best-hokkien-mee-singapore-2026 and the Michelin Guide entry for Come Daily. Other listings originated from Google Maps search links. The listings have not been independently field-verified.

Coordinates are approximate; addresses should be checked before travelling. Opening hours, prices, queues, availability and travel estimates are not live. The prototype does not yet support direct Google/Outlook calendar sync, accounts or server storage.
